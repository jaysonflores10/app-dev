# Attack on Titan

## About the Series

"Attack on Titan" is an anime / manga series created by Hajime Isayama about humanity's last survivors residing in an isolated island protected by 3 giant walls that protect them from tall, abnormal, and human-eating creatures called 'titans'. The series has a very dark and serious theme and it is full of amazing plot twists and backstories, which gives a lot of depth to the story's plot.

### Amazing facts about the series

1. The majority of the plot occurs on an island called 'Paradis Island' which is based in the real-life city of Nördlingen, a medieval town located in Bavaria, Germany.
2. Major characters are inspired by real-life inspirations such as the Armored Titan being inspired by WWE superstar and MMA fighter, Brock Lesnar, and the Attack Titan being inspired by Japanese MMA fighter, Yushin Okami.
3. The connections found within the plot are very well-written, with events from season 1 hinting at events in the final season, and vice versa.
4. The world map of the world in Attack on Titan is an upside down mirrored variant of the real world.
